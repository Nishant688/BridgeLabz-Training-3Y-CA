The Millennium Capital

Kyoto served as Japan's imperial capital for over a millennium. Today it remains the cultural heartbeat of the nation, boasting over two thousand temples, refined wooden townhouses, and serene rock gardens passed down through generations.

Kinkaku-ji & Sacred Shrines

From the radiant gold leaf pavilion of Kinkaku-ji reflecting upon the mirror pond to the thousands of vermillion torii gates winding up sacred Mount Inari, Kyoto's architecture embodies timeless harmony.

Living Traditions & Tea Ceremonies

Experience the profound quietude of a traditional tea ceremony in historic Gion, and savor multi-course Kaiseki cuisine curated to reflect the fleeting beauty of each season.

Arashiyama Bamboo Forest

Towering green stalks sway rhythmically in the wind, creating an acoustic and visual sanctuary celebrated as one of the natural soundscapes of Japan.
